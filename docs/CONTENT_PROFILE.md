# Pinned VALORANT demo content

The `valorant` profile uses Vandal and Spectre at 10 m, with the aggressive and flanker strategies. It is an illustrative **simulated body-shot duel**, not actual match data. The 10 m initial range and closing movement keep simulated encounters inside Spectre's 0–15 m damage band. Vandal's source band is 0–50 m. Fire rate is shots per second, magazine size is rounds, reload time is seconds, and damage is body damage per projectile.

The fixture and artwork are pinned from the unofficial [Valorant-API weapon catalog](https://valorant-api.com/v1/weapons), with version information from its [version endpoint](https://valorant-api.com/v1/version), retrieved 2026-11-14 UTC. The selected API records and version are in `fixtures/valorant_source_snapshot.json`; the full response hash and per-artwork source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the fixture and `web/assets/manifest.json`. Artwork is game content © Riot Games; Valorant-API is unofficial and is not endorsed by Riot Games. The dashboard focuses on VALORANT; the separate OpenFire snapshot remains available to legacy tools.

The simulator assumes a 100 HP target, no armor, no headshots, and a simplified independent hit probability. Its 1.3° spread, 0.3° aimed spread, 50 m model range, reactions and movement are assumptions, not values inferred from the API's first-bullet accuracy. It does not simulate abilities, precise recoil, movement penalties, map geometry, or the complete game's damage mechanics. The source's other distance bands are retained in the selected raw records but are not used in this profile. The engine's generic falloff is inactive at the profile's selected range.

To rebuild normalized data and the asset manifest from the pinned records and existing PNGs, run `python3 scripts/ingest_valorant.py` from the repository root. It performs no network request. Refreshing the source snapshot and images is a deliberate content update: obtain both public endpoints and images, record retrieval time and hashes, inspect changed units and damage bands, then rerun ingestion and tests. Do not silently relabel the OpenFire fixture as VALORANT content.

The dashboard permits bounded edits to both weapons’ body damage, fire rate, magazine size and reload time. These bounds are demo search limits, not official balance rules. Reload and magazine changes may have little effect in short duels.
