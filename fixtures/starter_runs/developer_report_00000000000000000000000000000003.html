Metric definitions

  • Win rate: A wins plus half of draws, divided by duels, with both starting orders paired for each seed.
  • Median TTK: median elimination time among decisive duels in each matchup; summary mean is the mean of matchup medians.
  • First-shooter advantage: absolute distance of each matchup's first-shooter win rate from 50%, averaged across matchups.
  • Maximum matchup spread: largest range between weapon win rates within any fixed distance and bot style.
  • Guardrails check configured neutral-matchup bands, first-shooter threshold, and off-target regression limit.
